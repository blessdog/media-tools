**This is a PLAN, not a finding. Do not build against it.**

A translucent band drifting on its own plane -- the multiplane technique from
The Old Mill (1937) -- would read as atmosphere, and the summits could carry it
without any ink being displaced.

But it puts material on screen that Wang Meng did not paint, and zero
fabrication is the whole pitch of this project. That makes it Ryan's call, not
an engineering one. It has not been made.

Recorded as `open` specifically because the last unproven plan in this project
(`drift`/mist as displacement) was inherited by a later session and built
against as though it were a specification. The type exists to stop that
happening twice.
