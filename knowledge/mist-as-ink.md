Measured across all three `drift` regions in `regions.json`:

    region              "ink" fraction   max stroke thickness   thin stroke
    m-far-peaks-blue        40.3%              59 px               2.4%
    m-summit-pocket         37.5%              61 px               2.7%
    m-compound-band         40.0%              75 px               2.4%

Forty percent ink with a 60-75px maximum thickness is not vapour. Displacing it
wobbles the silhouette of a mountain -- precisely the smear the inventory
feared, arrived at from the opposite direction.

Evidence: `jobs/wang-meng/living/evidence-mist-has-no-ink.png`.

The `drift` class is retired as an ink effect. An atmospheric mist CARD is a
separate, open question -- see `mist-card`.
