Local ink DENSITY plus ink COMPACTNESS inside an authored box, box grown 120px
for the read, components kept by centroid.

Colour cannot do this and it is measured three times: 0.9% of leaf ink is
green/cyan, cliff ink is MORE saturated than leaf ink, and in Lab the compound
canopies sit 1-3 units from bare cliff on both a and b.

Density alone claims 36% of the whole painting -- a dark wash band and a
shadowed cliff face are also "a lot of ink" -- so the authored box decides
WHERE to look. Compactness (boundary-per-ink) separates leaf from hatching:
leaf canopy 0.25, great-trees knoll 0.47, cliff wash 1.14, bare cliff 1.37.

**The scope is the whole point of this record.** This rule was recorded as
working, with no note of where, and a later session applied it two thousand
pixels up the scroll, where it claimed 36-46% of a summit crest box -- an
entire mountainside.
