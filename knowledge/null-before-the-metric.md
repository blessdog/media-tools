**Build the null before believing the number.** A static control, a synthetic
flat version, an untreated frame — state the null explicitly and construct it.
A metric with a plausible story attached is not evidence.

Measured floors on this job, to be quoted AGAINST rather than instead of:

    silk-survival drift floor    11%   (static control)
    hold static half             frame-to-frame change 0.0000

Three times this has been the deciding move, each time against a number that
already had a story:

- A parallax claim survived an hour of fluent prose and died 90 seconds after
  being rendered beside a synthetic pure zoom. Two different metrics had
  "proved" it.
- `clean-plate` reported texture energy 16.28 inside a hole against 16.35 in the
  ring and passed itself — while having synthesised orange autumn leaves where a
  blue-green pine was. A texture statistic cannot tell the right weave from the
  wrong content with the right weave. See [[clean-plate-donor-scope]].
- A tone-drift metric rated a cartoon excavator "inconclusive".

Migrated from `STATE.md` LAW 4. Sibling of [[rest-is-a-noop]]: that one is the
identity case for a TRANSFORM, this is the control case for a MEASUREMENT.
