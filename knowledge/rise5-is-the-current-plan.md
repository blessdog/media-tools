## READ `docs/plans/2026-08-25-rise5-plan.md` BEFORE DOING ANYTHING ON THE FILM

RISE 4 was rejected in full on 2026-08-25 and the shot list retired at tag
`hard-pivot-rise5-prep`. The one-unbroken-rise design written that morning was
retracted the same day and lives in
`archive/superseded-plans/2026-08-25-rise5-design-ONE-UNBROKEN-RISE.md`.

**The end state:** the mountain is alive and a camera explores it — wide, crop
in, pan around, zoom out, in and out, left, up, down, right. Under four minutes.
Rendered through **Blender**, not `render-parallax.py`.

**Phases, strictly ordered 0–2:**

```
0  THE CUT        bushels on every tree, not just Ge's
1  THE MOTION     scattered gentle events, varied direction
2  WHAT MOVES     sparse; ridge pines held; never one lone mover
3  THE WATERFALL  built already, never framed
4  THE SHOTS      Blender camera, differential >= 1.05x
5  ASSEMBLE
```

**Phase 0 is the blocker and nothing downstream is worth doing first**: gentle
motion applied to confetti is still confetti. Measured — identical settings,
`s-pine-over-bridge` 19 cards (12 hinged at a branch), `s-great-trees-upper` 147
cards with **113 hinged at an arbitrary foot**.

frequency (~3–4 per 8s, or a few per 20s?), and the marked contact sheets at
`jobs/wang-meng/evidence/weld/sheet-{0..3}.png`, cells 000 river → 169 summit.
