> "A card with a soft edge is still a card."

**Grading is not geometry.** Feathering, atmospheric tinting, blur-by-depth and
exposure falloff change how a plane READS; they do not change where it is. If a
stack looks flat because the planes are badly placed, no amount of edge softening
or aerial-perspective tinting will fix it — it will produce a soft flat picture.

The corollary is the useful half: when something reads as cut-out, ask whether
the defect is placement (geometry, fixable by re-planning — see
[[plan-planes-at-shot-scale]]) or appearance (grading, fixable in post). Only one
of those is a grading job.
