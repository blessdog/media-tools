## Gust envelope — wind as EVENT (build queue item 1, TECHNIQUES.md)

The one Disney technique we lacked: The Old Mill's wind arrives, bends
things base-first, passes, and the air goes calm. Our sway was a
metronome (`sin(t - lag)` forever).

**Built:** `--gust A,H,D` on animate-strokes (sway only) — attack/hold/
decay as fractions of the cycle; the front sweeps downwind along
`--angle` at `--gust-travel` px per cycle, so the bending travels across
the frame; `--gust-rest` keeps a faint idle sway between gusts (dead-still
foliage reads as a freeze); `--gust-push` adds the downwind bow at peak;
`--gust-flutter` sets oscillations inside the window. The envelope is
zero at both ends of its window → the loop still closes exactly.
Also fixed: `peakDisplacementPx` was sampled from the LAST drawing only —
for a gust cycle that's calm air; now a running max over all drawings.
Old flags render byte-identically (gust path adds only a `* 1.0` when off).

**Test (proven pine region, Z1 plate):** `--only pine-over-bridge --field
sway --pivot 798,1160 --stiffness 3 --frames 192 --on 2 --gust
0.10,0.08,0.22 --gust-travel 1500 --gust-rest 0.15 --gust-push 2.5` →
96 drawings, 8s loop. Leakage outside region 0.00 (self-test), peak 5.12px.

**Controls (adjacent-drawing activity in the ink region,
`living/plane-cycles/gust-activity.json`):**
- constant sway (null): flat 1.03–1.34 every frame — wind as state.
- gust cycle: calm floor 0.07–0.11 for ~60% of the loop, peak 1.34 as
  the front passes (same energy as the old sway, delivered as an event).
- loop seam 0.079 = calm-air adjacent level → no pop at the wrap.
- in the delivered A/B mp4 itself: right half dMean 0.188 during gust /
  0.029 in calm; left (constant) 0.16 always.

**Evidence:** `living/AB-GUST-VS-SWAY.mp4` (left = constant sway, right =
gust envelope), `living/evidence-gust-vs-constant-sway.png` (activity
curves). Opened both.

### Gust v1 FAILED on Ryan's eyes — smeared leaves. Mechanism + fix

Ryan: "I thought we already solved the leaves blowing gently in the wind.
These just look like a couple of them get smeared around and blurred."

Mechanism (two errors, both mine):
1. `--gust-push` v1 TRANSLATED the canopy bodily downwind (fx,fy uniform
   across space). The sway code's own comment bans exactly this: a sliding
   canopy "reads as a decal / the 2.5D sticker tell". Fix: the bow is now
   a ROTATION about the pivot — tangent displacement weighted by how
   downwind the tangent points — the same physics as the proven sway.
2. v1 flutter had no distance lag, so the whole canopy flapped in phase
   (rigid flapping). Fix: flutter reuses the cantilever lag term, tips lag.
Also switched the test to the class foliage params (wobble 3 drift 5) —
v1 ran tool defaults.

Sharpness control (Laplacian HF energy in the ink region vs source
plate): gust @peak now 64.5% of source — the PROVEN constant sway at its
own peak is 66.0%. Equal resampling cost, no extra smear. Calm frames
86.8%. Activity: peak 1.26 / calm 0.065 / seam 0.065 (no pop).
A/B rebuilt (left = proven sway, right = gust): in-clip dMean 0.182 vs
0.181 during the gust, 0.025 in calm air. Reopened for verdict.

## FLOAT-MIDSTREAM verdict: REGRESSION — Ryan is right. Mechanism found.

What we solved on 2026-08-14 (commit cfbb664, living-both-final.mp4):
- Working space was shot-real.png = 720×1280 — the master crop DOWNSCALED
  2.34:1. Wave params wobble 4 / drift 12 / λ250 are 720-SPACE pixels:
  drift = 1.7% of frame width, wavelength = 35% of frame width. The wave
  read as one current streaming THROUGH the whole surface.
- Content was the rapids/cascade — the densest water in the scroll —
  PLUS the tree swaying in the same shot ("both"). Measured: 2.5% of the
  frame visibly changes, organized as a coherent current.

What FLOAT-MIDSTREAM did instead:
- The SAME NUMBERS applied at native master resolution and viewed 1:1 in
  a 1920-wide window: drift = 0.6% of frame width (2.7× weaker), λ = 13%
  of frame width — isolated rings wiggle in place; no current.
- Content was the midstream pools — the CALMEST water Wang Meng painted:
  a countable handful of ripple rings on bare silk.
- No sway cycles exist yet, no gusts, no figures: 99.2% of every frame
  is a still (0.77% differs from a dead-still render vs the proven 2.5%,
  at a third of the relative amplitude).

The law this violates already exists as Law 2 for planes: PLAN AT SHOT
SCALE. Its motion analog was never written: **motion params are
shot-scale quantities, never master-px constants.** wobble/drift/λ must
be multiplied by k when the working resolution changes (×2.34 here), or
better, specified as fraction-of-frame per shot.

Compounding error: pointing the first probe at the quietest water. The
proven technique impresses where the ink is dense (rapids, cascade,
falls) and where the ensemble plays (water + foliage + gust together).
Pools are a garnish for held shots, not a demo.
