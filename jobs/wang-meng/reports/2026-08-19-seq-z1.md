## Gust envelope — wind as EVENT (build queue item 1, TECHNIQUES.md)

The one Disney technique we lacked: The Old Mill's wind arrives, bends
things base-first, passes, and the air goes calm. Our sway was a
metronome (`sin(t - lag)` forever).

**Built:** `--gust A,H,D` on animate-strokes (sway only) — attack/hold/
decay as fractions of the cycle; the front sweeps downwind along
`--angle` at `--gust-travel` px per cycle, so the bending travels across
the frame; `--gust-rest` keeps a faint idle sway between gusts (dead-still
foliage reads as a freeze); `--gust-push` adds the downwind bow at peak;
`--gust-flutter` sets oscillations inside the window. The envelope is
zero at both ends of its window → the loop still closes exactly.
Also fixed: `peakDisplacementPx` was sampled from the LAST drawing only —
for a gust cycle that's calm air; now a running max over all drawings.
Old flags render byte-identically (gust path adds only a `* 1.0` when off).

**Test (proven pine region, Z1 plate):** `--only pine-over-bridge --field
sway --pivot 798,1160 --stiffness 3 --frames 192 --on 2 --gust
0.10,0.08,0.22 --gust-travel 1500 --gust-rest 0.15 --gust-push 2.5` →
96 drawings, 8s loop. Leakage outside region 0.00 (self-test), peak 5.12px.

## Relief displacement: the LDI hybrid lands (commit 222ccd1)

Ryan's go-ahead ("yep. hit it") on the agreed next step: per-plane
relief, so a surface bulges as the camera passes instead of sliding as
a flat sheet — cards keep their completed edges, surfaces gain
continuous parallax WITHIN a card. This is the hybrid the literature
calls layered depth images.

**Where relief comes from, without breaking the depth law.** Monocular
scene depth stays dead (49–88% row-explained). What DAv2 is allowed to
contribute is local shape INSIDE one authored card: relief = its raw
depth minus a masked-normalized heavy blur (sigma = min-side/4), so the
scene-scale ramp — the exact measured failure — high-passes to zero by
construction. First build had a bright halo hugging every alpha edge
(unmasked blur mixed paper into the low-pass and the subtraction rang
at the boundary — displacement precisely where edges must stay
pinned); the masked blur killed it. Evidence: evidence-relief-maps.png
[crop | DAv2 raw | relief] — the cliff nose bulges, the foreground
boulders read as rounded forms.

**The figure check earned its keep.** Candidate planes eyeballed at
full res before enabling: left-bank-rocks carries a WOMAN,
resting-ledge carries THREE resting travelers, right-hill-front-trees
two travelers + ox, great-trees-knoll a robed figure in the vines.
All excluded — figures are sacred and never take displacement. The
roster is the three figure-free masses: left-cliff-wall,
gorge-wall-right (band 0.6 — at zr≈3.7 they need ~20x the band),
foreground-rock-mass (band 0.08 at zr=1.0).

**The renderer.** render-parallax --relief: each relief card's map
rides the plane's own transform into screen space, then the card is
radially remapped about the camera axis by the ratio of its screen
scales r = (zr+dz)(zr−camZ)/(zr(zr+dz−camZ)). camZ=0 → r ≡ 1: the
