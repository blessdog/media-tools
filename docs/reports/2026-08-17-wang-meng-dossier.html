The Wang Meng Dossier 葛稚川移居圖

Everything measured, killed, and proven in the campaign to fly a drone camera through a 14th-century hanging scroll — and how each finding constrains the next move. Written so no session, compaction, or API kill can lose the thread again. Companion to the always-current jobs/wang-meng/STATE.md.

01The mission

One continuous vertical video. The camera enters Wang Meng's scroll at the river mouth and travels the route Ge Hong's procession takes — eleven stations, bottom to top — experiencing the painting as terrain: z-axis travel, parallax, each region seen from the eye the painter gave it (散点透視: the river level, the gorge looked through, the peaks looked up at). Every rendered pixel is Wang Meng's. Depth is authored, never estimated. The Zhangjiajie drone footage is a feel reference only.

The master asset is a 6586×15923 (105 MP) scan. The world speaks master pixels; k = 2.34 master px per rendered px; output 720×1280.

02The laws — each one paid for

LAW 1 · DEPTH IS AUTHORED, NEVER ESTIMATED Monocular depth on this painting explains 55% of its variance with image row alone — it reads height-on-page, not distance; the cliff, gorge, and the entire trestle bridge are absent from its map. Exception, measured: pointed at a figure at native crop, the same model sculpts real volume (Ge relief 0.0698 vs a flat-card null of 0; corr with ink −0.064 — completing shape, not tracing brushwork). Depth where the object has a strong shape prior; convention where it does not.
LAW 2 · PLAN PLANES AT SHOT SCALE A scroll-scale stack seen through one 720×1280 window collapses to 3 planes with depth σ 0.098. Planned at shot scale the same window holds 13 planes, σ 0.394. Scroll stacks encode depth between shots, not within one.
LAW 3 · FRAME-ZERO CONTROL Every fill or extension must change 0 pixels with the camera at rest. Tonight's Z1 chain passed exactly: the flux fill's rest delta is 0 px (a 534-px hairline floor belongs to the card renderer itself and pre-exists the fill).
LAW 4 · NULL BEFORE ANY MOTION METRIC Three parallax "proofs" died to controls. The static control loses 11% on silk-survival — quote every result against that floor, never against 100%. A metric with a plausible story attached is not evidence.
LAW 5 · NAMING A SUBSTANCE REQUESTS FOOTAGE OF IT At cfg 6, "waterfall" replaced the painting with photographic water in ~2 seconds. The recipe that holds: camera-only positive prompt, photographic failure modes in the negative, cfg 2–3. Inside a mask that contains only water, the word "water" becomes safe.
LAW 6 · GRADING IS NOT GEOMETRY Blurring a plane stack feathers its seams; it does not make a surface. A card with a soft edge is still a card — measured: graded and flat maps opened identical holes under a push.
LAW 7 · SEAL, PIN, AND SHARE YOUR GROUND Unclaimed ink lands on the base plane one step past the farthest card and is magnified at two rates at once — Ge was split 81/13 and visibly tore. Seal by proximity (never by component: one ink component is 17.7% of all ink), pin objects whole, and every figure rides its supporting surface's depth. Each rule is checkable as code, and tonight the point-in-mask invariant caught two masks the coverage numbers had blessed.
LAW 8 · JUDGE THE OUTPUT, NOT THE ARCHITECTURE Slop is a property of pixels, not of a model's category — but never silently downgrade, name the trade in the manifest, and the eyes on the file are the verdict. Corollary from tonight: a deterministic tool outside its stated contract produces garbage as surely as a cheap model (see warp, §05).

06The rangefinder — the painter measured the journey for us

Wang Meng paints one class of person at near-constant real size, so painted height is distance: h ∝ 1/z. Reading the servants across the scroll (tools/range-planes.py, committed):

WitnessStationHeight, master pxz (bank ≡ 1)
ox-leader on the bank2 · Z15151.00
traveler on the fenced cliff path6 · Z32552.02
figure at the compound9 · Z51703.03

The journey is spaced in clean multiples — the cliff path twice the entry distance, the compound three times. And the instrument caught its own trap: Ge measures 1.48× a servant standing nearer than him. Two scales at once — hierarchical (status) scale rides on top of optical scale, so protagonists never calibrate. Within one zone the class height spread exceeds the z separation; the rangefinder is a cross-zone instrument. These three numbers price the zone worlds' spacing and define camera speed in real units.

07The Voyager gate — every guard traced to its source

HunyuanWorld-Voyager: single image + user camera trajectory → world-consistent RGB-D video, conditioned on partial renders of a point cloud. Architecturally it is our own loop — render the known, complete only the unknown — run inside the one model family measured to like this ink. Recon findings and the design each one forces:

Landscape bridge crop, composed from the master Voyager force-resizes input to 1280×720 landscape; feeding a vertical crop would squash the painting before the model ever sees it.
Swap MoGe's estimated depth for our authored composed depth in create_input.py Law 1: estimated depth on this painting is a row-reading ramp — garbage conditions in, invention out. The script is plain Python; the depth tensor is substitutable. Run 1 still establishes the stock baseline first.
Camera-only positive prompt, standard negative list, default guidance Law 5: naming a substance requests footage of it.
Full-weight official fp16 checkpoints, 50 steps, no substitution on failure — stop and report Law 8 and the never-ship-slop covenant: silent downgrade is the sin; a pipeline proven with slop proves nothing.
Pre-registered pass/fail before rendering: silk-survival ≥ the Hunyuan-fix1 band (~65% against the 11% control floor), no invented objects, figures not redrawn; judged on a side-by-side evidence page against the source and the tilted-cards render of the same push Law 4: build the null first. The Wan excavator was caught exactly this way.
Dry-run rent quote first; $2.20/hr cap; $10 total budget; provision script committed before renting; 45-minute provisioning kill criterion; destroy on completion gpu-box's cost-safe design plus the evening's directive: no diving in headfirst with someone else's money.
Run 2 (authored-depth conditions) fires only if Run 1's ink holds Stop-fix-verify: never buy the second experiment on hope for the first.

What a pass buys: Voyager takes over the camera pushes with true model-rendered dimensionality, fed by our authored world. What a fail buys: a clean boundary, ~$5, and the tilted-cards path continues unharmed — it is already watchable.
